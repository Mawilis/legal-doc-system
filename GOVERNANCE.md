# Governance

WILSY OS Core Governance owns repository policy and certification posture.
Python EOS owns sovereign business and intelligence truth. Every artifact must
identify authority, tenant boundary, evidence boundary, and verification
limits.

The public status artifact is descriptive metadata rendered deterministically;
it cannot establish compliance, approval, authorization, execution, payment,
settlement, or production readiness. Generated catalogs are informational and
never outrank executable source or persisted evidence.

Changes should be narrowly scoped, independently verifiable, and left
unstaged until reviewed. Historical release tags are retained as legacy
markers.
