## 1. RepositoryEvidence

The G61 repository investigation confirmed:

- Canonical current-tenant IAM, exact permission-operation binding and active
  authorization-role checks already exist in Python/EOS.
- Conflict screening is a signal, not practitioner clearance.
- Human conflict review produces firm-level review evidence only.
- Conflict disposition establishes engagement context, not practitioner access.
- Practitioner binding is relationship evidence, not content permission.
- G56 clearance registry stores documentary, unverified event history.
- G60 confidentiality-review decision is immutable but non-authorizing.
- G23 operational issuer remains explicitly fail-closed.

Institutional reviewer IAM ownership, wall-policy authority, actual
enforcement, acknowledgement provenance and emergency-denial ownership
have not been certified.

## 3. Mandatory safety invariants

1. Default deny if reviewer IAM, source policy, wall enforcement or current
   transaction-bound evidence is unavailable.
2. A practitioner must not approve their own matter clearance.
3. Approval and wall administration require separately evidenced authority.
   Whether the same person may hold both roles requires institutional approval.
4. A determination of NO_WALL_REQUIRED requires independent policy provenance;
   a reviewer assertion or caller boolean is insufficient.
5. WALL_REQUIRED demands effective current membership, acknowledgements where
   prescribed, and enforcement-source verification.
6. Conflict-denied, review-denied, revoked, expired, stale or contradictory
   states block access notwithstanding any recorded positive evidence.
7. Durable positive history cannot override a current explicit denial.
8. All reads and writes must preserve exact tenant, matter and practitioner
   scope; source revalidation is required at the access-admission boundary.
9. SHA3-512 proves deterministic content consistency, not identity or
   reviewer authentication.
10. No financial, billing, AI or cross-tenant authority is created here.
