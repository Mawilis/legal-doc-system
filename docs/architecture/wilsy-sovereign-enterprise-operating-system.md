## Target sovereign architecture

The target lifecycle is **DISCOVER → MAP → WIRE → CLEAN → CERTIFY → OBSERVE →
INTELLIGENCE → EVOLVE**. Canonical domain services own state transitions and
immutable evidence. Registries provide tenant-scoped durable truth with
idempotency, optimistic concurrency, and caller-owned transaction boundaries.
Kennel EOS exclusively owns financial execution; approval, release authority,
execution, and settlement remain distinct states.

External providers, transport, standards, and integrations are replaceable
adapters. Client surfaces are projections. Evidence and telemetry are derived
from canonical commits and cannot manufacture success. Security, tenant
isolation, deterministic fingerprints, and fail-closed error taxonomies apply
across every boundary.

## Migration path

1. **Discover:** inventory routes, schemas, persistence, clients, tests,
   integrations, intelligence consumers, and legacy paths.
2. **Map:** assign canonical owner, lifecycle, dependencies, callers,
   projections, authority boundaries, and migration source for each capability.
3. **Wire:** connect canonical services to clients, evidence, observability,
   and intelligence through explicit adapters and tenant-scoped contracts.
4. **Clean:** quarantine or retire duplicate/overlapping authorities only after
   replacement behavior and durable evidence are certified.
5. **Certify/Observe:** require static, unit, integration, and real-database
   proofs where applicable; publish truthful telemetry and evidence.
6. **Intelligence/Evolve:** derive analytics and recommendations from owned
   truth, then evolve through versioned, reversible, evidence-backed changes.

## Legal Operations & Process Service Plane

Legal Operations owns process-service operational truth: `LegalInstruction`,
`ProcessDocument`, `DocumentCustodyEvent`, `ServiceAttempt`,
`ServiceExecution`, `ReturnOfService`, and `TariffAssessment`. Billing owns
invoice truth; Kennel EOS owns financial execution; Settlement owns allocation;
the Ledger owns accounting postings; Intelligence Dock owns no canonical
operational truth. This plane connects to identity, evidence, workflow, AR,
notifications, search, files, mobile/edge, observability, Storefront, and
tenant genesis without duplicating entity ownership.
